# Reader profile

The reader is Gero: a chemical engineer doing an MBA at Berkeley Haas and an MEng in materials science at the same time, aiming his career at the energy transition. Before Berkeley he spent four years at Mastercard Advisors building digital-payments programs across Latin America. He builds small AI tools himself (he trained a language model from scratch and built multi-agent pipelines), so he reads AI news as a practitioner, not a spectator. He lives in the Bay Area and follows the US and Mexico closely.

## Clean tech (highest interest)
- Batteries and storage: chemistries (sodium-ion, solid-state, LFP), materials, manufacturing, grid-scale storage, supply chains for lithium, nickel, graphite.
- Grid and power: transmission, interconnection queues, utilities, power prices, data-centre load growth, nuclear (SMRs, restarts), geothermal (enhanced geothermal), solar and wind deployment.
- Hydrogen, electrolysis, e-fuels, industrial decarbonisation (steel, cement, chemicals), carbon removal.
- Climate tech companies: funding rounds, failures, IPOs, big offtake deals; policy that moves money (IRA credits, tariffs, permitting).
- Prefers substance: numbers, costs, capacity, who is building what. Skips listicles, product reviews and EV deal roundups.

## AI (high interest)
- Frontier model releases and capabilities, from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Meta and open-weight labs.
- Research that changes practice: training, evaluation, agents, interpretability, safety.
- AI and energy: data-centre power demand, chips, compute build-out.
- Business of AI: major funding, pricing, regulation (US, EU), and how companies actually deploy it.
- Skips: gadget AI features, celebrity takes, hype pieces without substance.

## World (must know)
- Anything a well-informed person is expected to know today: wars and peace talks, elections, major disasters, big economic moves (Fed, inflation, markets, trade).
- The US: federal politics and policy with real consequences, California and the Bay Area.
- Mexico: politics, the economy, US–Mexico relations (trade, tariffs, migration, security), nearshoring, energy (Pemex, CFE).
- Latin America when it is big news (he worked across the region).
- Skips: celebrity, sports results, crime stories without wider significance, opinion columns.
